## 1. Research summary

### Market (what's already ranking)
Top competing gig titles all stack the same keywords: **3D medical animation,
surgical animation, medical device animation, anatomy, explainer video, 4K**.
Examples:
- "I will do 3d medical animation surgery anatomy medical device science explainer video"
- "I will create 4k 3d medical animation, 3d product demo surgery procedure, explainer video"
- "I will create 3d medical device animation for medtech and healthcare companies"
- "I will create 3d medical animation surgical procedure orthopedic medical device video"

Gaps to exploit:
1. Most gigs are generic. Few name a **buyer type** (MedTech startups, device
   sales reps, pharma MOA, patient education) or a **niche** (orthopedic/spine,
   cardiovascular/catheter, dental, ophthalmic).
2. Almost no one sells **"AI-assisted" fast-turnaround explainers** next to
   premium 3D. That's a cheaper Basic tier that converts first-time buyers.
3. Many gigs say "message me before ordering" with no visible price, so a clear,
   fixed Basic package wins impatient buyers.

Price benchmark outside Fiverr: studios charge roughly $8k–$18k for a device
animation and $15k–$40k+ for surgical animation per finished minute. Fiverr
buyers come for a fraction of that, so you can price well above typical
"cheap animation" gigs and still look like a bargain.

### Fiverr SEO (ranking on Fiverr search)
- The primary keyword goes **in the title, early** ("3D medical animation").
- Use **all 5 tags**, each a real search phrase, no repeats of the same word set.
- Repeat the main keywords naturally in the **description, package names, FAQ
  and image/video file names**.
- Ranking after launch depends on **click-through (thumbnail), conversion,
  response time, on-time delivery and reviews**. Answer messages within an hour
  for the first weeks.
- A **gig video** is essential in this category: a 30–60 s showreel.
- New gigs usually take 2–8 weeks to settle. Don't edit the title/tags every
  day; refresh images or tweak price if impressions stall after ~3 weeks.

### AEO (Answer Engine Optimization)
Buyers now ask Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT and Perplexity things like *"how
much does a 3D surgical animation cost?"*. Answer engines pull short, direct
Q&A text, so:
- Write the gig **FAQ as the exact questions buyers ask**, and answer in the
  first sentence with a number or a yes/no.
- Put the same Q&A on your portfolio site with **FAQPage schema**.

### GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)
To be the seller an AI assistant *recommends*:
- Use **one consistent name + phrase everywhere**: "[Your Name] – 3D Medical &
  Surgical Animator" on Fiverr, LinkedIn, YouTube, Vimeo, ArtStation, Behance
  and your site.
- Publish each portfolio piece on **YouTube/Vimeo with descriptive titles**
  ("3D Spinal Fusion Surgery Animation – Pedicle Screw Placement") and link to
  your Fiverr gig in the description.
- A simple portfolio page with clear **service + niche + price-from** text,
  Organization/Person schema, and an `llms.txt` file listing your services.
- Get **mentions on third-party pages** (MedTech forums, LinkedIn articles,
  guest posts, directories). Generative engines lean on sources that cite you.
- Collect reviews that **name the procedure/device** ("great knee implant
  animation"). Those phrases get quoted.

No source shows exactly how AI tools pick freelancers. Treat GEO as a set of
good bets, not a guarantee.

### FAQ (written for AEO)
**How much does a 3D medical animation cost?**
Packages start at $150 for a 15-second device spin and $1,200 for a 60-second
4K cinematic animation. Longer or complex procedures get a custom quote.

**Can you animate my medical device from CAD files?**
Yes. I accept STEP, STL, OBJ, FBX and SolidWorks exports. No CAD? I can model
from photos, drawings or the IFU.

**Are your surgical animations medically accurate?**
I build each scene from your references, and you or your clinical team approve
the storyboard before animation. Final clinical sign-off stays with you.

**Do you use AI?**
The Basic AI explainer uses AI tools for speed and cost. Standard and Premium
are hand-built 3D animations. I always tell you which tools were used.

**How long does a surgical animation take?**
Most 30–60 second procedure animations take 8–14 days, including storyboard
approval and revisions.

**Can you sign an NDA?**
Yes. I'm happy to sign an NDA for unreleased devices.

**Who owns the final video?**
You get full commercial rights after the order is completed.

### Requirements (questions buyers answer on order)
1. What device, procedure or anatomy should the animation show? (required)
2. Who is the audience: patients, surgeons, investors or sales? (required)
3. Upload CAD files, IFU, images, reference videos or surgeon notes. (attachment)
4. Script or key messages, if you have them.
5. Preferred style: realistic, clean/white "Apple-style", or X-ray/translucent.
6. Brand colors, logo and end card text.

## 3. Compliance notes
- Only show work you own or are allowed to share. Never use a client's
  unreleased device in your portfolio without written permission.
- Don't put medical or efficacy claims in your own copy. You visualize; the
  client owns clinical claims and regulatory review.
- Mark AI-generated work honestly, as Fiverr requires.
